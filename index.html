    How CGPA is worked out

    Each course's grade points × its credits, added up and divided by total credits: Σ (grade points × credits)Σ credits

    An F counts as zero until you clear it.

    How average marks (WAM) are worked out

    The same calculation using marks instead of grade points: Σ (marks × credits)Σ credits

    For retakes, your better CLA score and your better final exam score are kept separately and added together, which is how Moodle does it. A lower retake score never pulls your mark down.
